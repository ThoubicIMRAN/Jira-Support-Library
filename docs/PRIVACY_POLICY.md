# Privacy Policy – Jira Support Library

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. Overview
Jira Support Library is a self-hosted internal application. All data processed and stored by this application resides exclusively within your organisation's internal database. The application developers and code owners do not have access to your data, servers, or database instances.

## 2. Information We Collect
To facilitate user authentication and support operations, the application collects and stores only the minimum required information:
- **Account Information:** Full name and email address.
- **Security Credentials:** Cryptographically hashed passwords (plain-text passwords are never stored).
- **Session Data:** Temporary session tokens used to maintain your logged-in state.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Local Hosting:** All user records and operational logs remain strictly within your internal infrastructure.
- **Session Management:** Session tokens are stored securely and expire automatically after a set period of inactivity.
- **Zero External Access:** Because the application is self-hosted, no telemetry, user records, or database contents are accessible to the code owners or external maintainers.

## 4. Data Sharing
No personal or operational data is sold, transmitted, or shared with third parties, external analytics services, or cloud providers outside your organisation's designated hosting environment.

## 5. Your Rights and Data Deletion
You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting your internal system administrator.

## 6. Contact Information
For privacy-related questions, data deletion requests, or technical support, please contact your internal **IT Operations** team.
